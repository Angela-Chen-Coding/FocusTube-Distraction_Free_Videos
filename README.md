# FocusTube-Distraction Free Videos!
Welcome to FocusTube - Your Personal Video Player!

This is a beautiful, distraction-free video manager that lets you watch, organize, and share videos from YouTube, TikTok, Vimeo, Twitch, Dailymotion, and more—all in one place!

**✨ Features:**
- 📚 **Build Your Library** - Save videos with custom names & descriptions
- 📝 **Smart Note-Taking** - Add timestamped notes that jump to that moment (YouTube)
- 📱 **Instant Sharing** - Generate QR codes or shareable links in one click
- 🎨 **Personalize** - 6 beautiful themes + dark mode
- 💾 **Export Everything** - Save your entire collection with a single code
- 🚫 **Zero Distractions** - No recommendations, no autoplay, just your videos

Perfect for students, researchers, content curators, or anyone who wants a cleaner way to manage their favorite videos!
